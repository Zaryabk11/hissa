You don’t inherently need to set it. In this project, [`start.mjs`](</Users/sobanahmad/Work/8x hackathon/hissa/frontend/scripts/start.mjs>) makes Expo advertise your Mac’s `.local` hostname by default. If your phone can’t resolve or reach that hostname on Wi‑Fi, setting `REACT_NATIVE_PACKAGER_HOSTNAME=192.168.100.232` tells it to use your Mac’s local network IP instead.

The `--clear` part is separate: [`package.json`](</Users/sobanahmad/Work/8x hackathon/hissa/frontend/package.json>) runs the start script, which forwards `--clear` to Expo to clear Metro’s cache. It doesn’t require the hostname setting.

Your [`frontend/.env`](</Users/sobanahmad/Work/8x hackathon/hissa/frontend/.env>) also has that hostname line commented out, so it isn’t active there. The inline prefix sets it only for that command. Try starting without it; if your phone can’t connect, use the IP—but the IP may change when you reconnect to Wi‑Fi.
