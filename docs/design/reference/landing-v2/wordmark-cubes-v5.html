Wordmark cubes: small spaced-out cubes at the cursor, big tight ones far away

Move across the word. It plays a short demo sweep on load.

Hover over the word.

Trigger: the effect only starts when the cursor is within 5px of the lettering (the white fill or its coloured shadow). Move into empty space and it fades out. How it behaves: the word is cut into square cubes that keep subdividing the closer they are to the cursor, from 240px blocks far away down to 30px cubes right at it. Near the cursor each cube shrinks inside its cell, so there is a big gap between neighbours, and tumbles by a few degrees. Far away the cubes fill their cells, so the gaps are tiny and the word still reads. Leaving the word makes the cubes grow back together.
