## MASTER PROMPT (my design principles, apply always)
Visual hierarchy (not F-pattern; biggest = most important). Solid high-contrast CTAs, never ghost buttons. Palette 60-30-10, check WCAG contrast. Typography: clear H1/H2, readable body, consistent. Conversion-focused: clarity, scannability, motivation; reduce friction. Design for the real audience (desi families). Treat design as hypotheses. A beautiful page that fails to convert is a failed page.

## MY DIRECTION

## LOCKED SO FAR

Assets: `assets/karahi-1024.jpg` (chicken karahi + rotis in gingham cloth, flat butter-yellow bg) and `assets/thali-1024.jpg` (overhead steel thali). Background is keyed to transparent in a shader (distance from corner-sampled bg colour). Brought to life with WebGL: steam waver, curry shimmer, cursor tilt. A Seedance video loop was tested (640px, soft, 1.9MB): decided against it as default.
**Hero (mirrored bento):** painting bottom-left, width 52.7% of panel, left -7%, bottom -9%, then shifted 80px right and 10px up (pinned chips moved with it). Wordmark top-right (canvas 1200x520, 50% width, right 1.5%, top 15%). Right-aligned text bottom-right: H1 "One more roti is fine.", body "Snap your plate. Hissa tells you your portion in roti and katori. Cook, eat and cheer on your family and friends.", green CTA "Get Hissa". Chips (cream, ink border, tail): portion chip pinned on the roti ("1½ roti, 1 katori / about 520 kcal · estimate"), plus a feed of notifications floating up from the steam one at a time (Ayesha logged lunch, Bilal cheered your plate, Zara joined your table; names are placeholders), 9s loop with 3s stagger.
**Wordmark effect:** liquid warp was replaced by **cube chop**: WebGL adaptive quadtree, cells 240/120/60/30px, subdivide within 1.5x cell size of the cursor; gap = .03+.26*i, tumble .5 rad*i, intensity falloff radius 140px (canvas px); jitter inside gap; no AA seam when idle. Triggers only when the cursor is within 5 CSS px of any lettering pixel (alpha>24 incl. shadow layers). Short auto sweep on load (3.2s). Respects reduced motion (static). Rendering pauses when covered. Reference code: `mockups/wordmark-cubes-v5.html`.
**Navbar:** black pill (#1a0a05) with 4 links in cream, liquid goo blob that stretches to the hovered link (two blobs, second lags, SVG goo filter, blur 7, matrix 22/-9), blob = linear-gradient(100deg, #0B6B3A, #C8102E), hovered text white. Logo حصہ small left, solid green CTA right. Link order follows page order: **Buddy, How it works, Features, FAQ**. Hybrid plate-on-scroll and radial ring gradient were rejected.
**Section order:** Hero, The idea, Buddy, How it works, Features, Dish rail, FAQ, Final CTA + footer with giant حصہ.
