## v3 outline (remaining)

1. **Learning from the user:** migration for `user_avoids` and `dishes.protein_type` (spec section 7 of the AI backend design); onboarding avoid-list endpoints in `profiles`; hard filters applied in code.
2. **Recipes (stretch):** needs an open recipe set with ingredients and steps first (spec section 9); `recipes` and `recipe_feedback` tables; deterministic ranking; one-tap "not for me". No model training for personalisation.

Already delivered by v2 and no longer part of v3: the 90-minute feel check-in and its screen. Still to come in v2: the weekly pattern rule (one answer in 60% or more of a category's check-ins, at least 3 check-ins, copy says "pattern", never "cause") and the Gemini-drafted follow-up question.
