# Hissa landing: brief

**Self-authored under explicit creative delegation.** The user said: "Design a beautiful landing page... Understand the project if you need to and execute." No interview was held. Evidence = repo (`docs/PRD.md`, `landing/content.ts`, `landingTokens.ts`, `CLAUDE.md` product rules) and the user's own 40-frame plate sequence (`frontend/assets/landing/hissa-frames.zip`). Assumptions are marked (A).

## The eight topics
1. **Vibe:** warm, quiet, unhurried, a dastarkhwan at night. Graphite ground, one lime accent (the napkin in the user's own frames). Refs (A): a late-night chai stall, a Wes Anderson overhead table shot, Muji packaging.
2. **Journey:** see the plate, hear that it's fine, learn the three taps, see the portions, cut your own share.
3. **Energy:** calm start, one lift at the turn, calm rail, the close is the quiet peak.
4. **Feeling:** appetite > relief > ease > curiosity > ownership. **Peak:** "I dragged the plate and it told me my share in roti and katori."
5. **Never-seen move (A):** the plate is a dial. You cut your own hissa and it answers in roti and katori.
6. **Range:** editorial-warm, premium-minimal with food photography. Not brutalist.
7. **Unbroken world or scenes:** scenes (one scrubbed plate film, then distinct acts).
8. **Assets:** the 40-frame overhead plate sequence (full-bleed scrub + card crops). Nothing generated, no spend.

## Product rules honoured
Every number is labelled an estimate. No medical claims, no invented stats, no counters. Friends/buddy detail omitted from the page (feature is not the story here). CTA label everywhere: **Explore Hissa** (store URL is still an empty placeholder in `content.ts`).

## Grammar: filmic one-shot
Why the other seven lost: editorial forbids the scrub hero we own; live surface would paint the app we cannot run here; continuous world has no geography; poster throws away the plate; gallery has no single arc; split stage has no two sides; cutlist is the opposite of "unhurried".

## Feeling curve
| Act | Feeling | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| 1 scrub | appetite, then wonder | the plate lifts apart under the hand |
| 2 pin | relief | "Your hissa is your share" assembles line by line while a ring fills |
| 3 flow + reveal | ease | three taps, one wipe to the plate |
| 4 pan | curiosity | katori by katori, real crops of the plate |
| 5 pin + pointer (PEAK) | ownership | you cut the plate yourself; largest span; silence before it |

Authored silence: the end of act 2 holds on one line with no motion.

## Signature move
**The plate dial.** SVG plate, wedge set by pointer angle, scroll sweeps it first, arrow keys work (slider role). Readout converts share to roti and katori, labelled an estimate.

## Tell-someone sentence
"It's the site where you cut your own share of the plate."
